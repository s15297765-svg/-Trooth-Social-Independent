# Trooth Social Independent

A light-green, Facebook-style social and business networking web app with dedicated spaces for News, Sports, Stores, Property, Business, Groups, and the combined Film & Fashion Story Space.

## Live site

https://s15297765-svg.github.io/-Trooth-Social-Independent/

## Current release notes

- Home resilience keeps major hubs from being left on a blank/loading state.
- Login/Profile flow uses normal account access; OTP is not part of the current release.
- GitHub Pages deployment is handled through the repository's Actions workflow.
- Supabase powers authentication, social content, realtime updates, and related app data.

## Main spaces

Home · Friends & Following · Business · News · Sports · Stores · Property · Film & Fashion · Groups · Messages · Notifications · Saved · Activity · Settings · Profile/Login
